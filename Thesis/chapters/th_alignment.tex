\mychapter{Alignment to National Imperatives}{Alignment to National Imperatives}{}
\label{chap:alignmenttonationalimperatives}

This research project aligns closely with South Africa’s strategic objectives to foster innovation in the Fourth Industrial Revolution (4IR) while addressing local challenges through cost-effective engineering solutions.

\section{Classification in line with NRF Broad Categories}{Classification in line with NRF Broad Categories}
\label{sec:nrf_classification}

\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{Engineering:}
    The core objective of this research, optimizing camera placement through simulation, directly supports the development of robust, deployable engineering systems. By mathematically determining the most efficient sensor configurations, this work enables the propagation of markerless pose estimation into non-laboratory environments. This facilitates ``frugal engineering,'' allowing diverse sectors (e.g., construction safety monitoring, industrial ergonomics) to implement high-fidelity motion tracking without the prohibitive costs of traditional ``gold-standard'' systems.

    \item \textbf{Computer and Information Sciences:}
    This project advances the field of computer vision by establishing a modular, synthetic data generation pipeline. By leveraging the SMPL-X body model and automated 3D-to-2D projection matrices, the research mitigates the ``data bias'' often prevalent in AI models trained on homogeneous datasets. Creating synthetic datasets that account for diverse body shapes and occlusion scenarios contributes directly to the \textit{Data Science and Artificial Intelligence} imperative, ensuring that South African algorithms are robust, inclusive, and globally competitive.
\end{enumerate}

\section{National Priorities}
\label{sec:national_priorities}

\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{Job Creation and High-End Skills Development:}
    The advancement of markerless motion capture technologies requires a workforce skilled in scarce critical fields, specifically data science, software engineering, and computational biomechanics. This research project not only produces a technical artifact but also contributes to human capacity development by training a postgraduate student in advanced simulation and optimization techniques. Furthermore, the commercialization potential of low-cost motion capture creates opportunities for new service-based economies, such as remote ergonomic auditing and digital personal training.
\end{enumerate}

\section{National Strategy: African Union Agenda 2063 (AU 2063)}
\label{sec:au2063}

\begin{enumerate}

    \item \textbf{Aspiration 2: An Integrated Continent, Politically United and based on the Ideals of Pan-Africanism and the Vision of Africa’s Renaissance.}
    
    \textit{Goal: Well-Educated Citizens and Skills Revolution underpinned by Science, Technology and Innovation.}
    
    This research contributes to the democratization of technology in education. Markerless pose estimation can serve as the foundation for interactive STEM educational tools, where students engage with physics and biology concepts through body movement. By lowering the hardware barrier to entry, this technology can be deployed in resource-constrained schools, narrowing the digital divide and fostering a tech-literate youth population capable of driving Africa’s future knowledge economy.
\end{enumerate}

\mysection{Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs)}{Sustainable Development Goals}
\label{sec:sdgs}

\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{Goal 3: Good Health and Well-being:}
    Musculoskeletal disorders are a significant burden on the healthcare system. Traditional gait analysis and biomechanical assessments are often restricted to specialized clinics. This research acts as an enabler for \textit{mHealth (Mobile Health)} by validating that optimal data can be captured using minimal, low-cost cameras. This facilitates remote tele-rehabilitation and consultation, significantly improving access to specialized healthcare for rural or underserved populations.

    \item \textbf{Goal 8: Decent Work and Economic Growth:}
    Beyond healthcare, markerless pose estimation is a critical component of the ``Smart Factory.'' By integrating optimized camera systems into industrial workspaces, companies can automatically monitor employee ergonomics to prevent repetitive strain injuries and identify hazardous behaviors in real-time. This promotes safer working environments and supports economic growth through increased productivity and reduced occupational health costs.

    \item \textbf{Goal 4: Quality Education:}
    As emphasized in the AU 2063 strategy, this technology enables novel pedagogical methods. Interactive, movement-based learning systems (gamified learning) rely on robust pose estimation. By optimizing these systems to work with standard webcams rather than expensive sensors, this research makes advanced educational aids accessible to a broader demographic, helping to bridge the information gap between privileged and under-resourced educational institutions.
\end{enumerate}
